Just a Scratch

  • ChapterChapter 1: Just Like Paradise
  • Mission number7 of 122
  • ProtagonistJason Duval

Jason takes his beat-up Ganado to Joey at Coastal Auto Care for the repairs it badly needs.

May contain spoilers.

Community

Mission guide

  • Alternate name: Fix Your Car
  • Who plays it: Jason only

Step by step

1. Drive the Ganado. Take the '70 Ganado to Joey's garage at Coastal Auto Care.

2. Repair and respray. Joey fixes the engine for free. Then pick any color to respray it.

Tips

  • If you wreck the car on the way, save and reload to get it back.

Reward

Free engine repair and a new paint job.
