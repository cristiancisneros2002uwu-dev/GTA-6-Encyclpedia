Moses

Story mission 35 · Chapter 2: Paradise Lost

Moses

  • ChapterChapter 2: Paradise Lost
  • Mission number35 of 122
  • ProtagonistLucia Caminos

Vicki said a mechanic named Moses might get Lucia a ride, so she suggested going to his shop in an inconspicuous car.

May contain spoilers.

Community

Mission guide

  • Alternate name: Take A Vehicle to Moses
  • Who plays it: Lucia only

Step by step

1. Follow Moses. Go with Moses to the yard.

2. Scan. Scan three cars with WAiNK (hold D-pad up).

3. Slim Jim. Take the Slim Jim and break into the Montagne XL.

4. Back. Drive it to the garage.

Tips

  • Respray a car before registering it as personal; garages have limited slots.
  • Trackers: none, standard or advanced.
  • Selling untracked cars to Moses is quick early money; he rejects cars stolen near his garage.

Reward

Slim Jim, Scooter Bros app, a free car and car sales to Moses.
