Lab Fees

Story mission 6 · Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Lab Fees

  • ChapterChapter 1: Just Like Paradise
  • Mission number6 of 122
  • ProtagonistJason Duval

Jason collects Brian’s rent at a gift shop that hides more than souvenirs.

May contain spoilers.

Official

Mission guide

  • Start: Mariana County › Key Lento · Given by: Jason · Requirement: finish Gone Fishing · Reward: $600
  • Alternate name: Collect Brian’s Rent
  • Who plays it: Jason only

Objectives

  1. Talk to the clerk.
  2. Get into the lab and collect the acid.
  3. Call Brian.
  4. Store the acid in Brian’s lockup.

Step by step

1. The store. Jason goes to a souvenir store that is really the front for an acid lab, to collect Brian’s rent. Use the phone and call Brian; he’ll tell you the clerk doesn’t have the rent money.

2. The lab. Since there’s no money, Jason takes product instead: go into the lab and collect the acid.

3. Call Brian. With the product in hand, call Brian again from the phone.

4. Brian’s lockup. It is marked with a green icon on the map. The door code is 7700. Inside, leave the acid in the stash to finish.

Tip

You can sell stolen narcotics, jewelry and marked bills to “fences” like Brian. More fences unlock as the story progresses and show up with a green icon on the map, just like the lockup.

Note

The lab door code in the store is 0420.
