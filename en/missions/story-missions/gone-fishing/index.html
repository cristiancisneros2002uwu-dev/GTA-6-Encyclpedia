Gone Fishing

Story mission 2 · Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise

Confirmation levelOfficialVisualSeen in demosCommunityUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Gone Fishing

  • ChapterChapter 1: Just Like Paradise
  • Mission number2 of 122
  • ProtagonistJason Duval

A smuggling attempt with Cal and Donnie goes wrong, and Jason has to venture into Kingfishers territory in Pelican Valley.

May contain spoilers.

Community

Mission guide

  • Start: Mariana County · Given by: Cal · Reward: Jason’s house in Key Lento
  • Who plays it: Jason only

Objectives

  1. Follow the plane and pick up the packages.
  2. Look for Donnie at the camp.
  3. Find a way into the cabin and untie Donnie.
  4. Head to the plane.
  5. Board the seaplane.
  6. Fly back to Brian’s marina.

Step by step

1. Packages at sea. After the opening cutscene, chase Donnie’s plane by boat and slow down near the packages so Cal can fish them out. Then follow the pink line on the minimap to the swamps and use the binoculars (weapon wheel) to watch the deal at the docks: the Kingfishers capture Donnie.

2. Get into the camp. Leave the boat at the nearby docks; Jason goes on alone. You can move in stealth: crouch, approach each enemy from behind, grab them and knock them out. Cross the camp to the cabin at the back, where they are holding Donnie.

3. Untie Donnie. Go in through the front door, avoiding the guard (he walks away after a while), and untie Donnie. A brute arrives: knock him out with your fists and block his punches. Then pick up the Zombix (healing item) and equip a weapon.

4. To the plane. You can now use weapons freely. On the way, the white and red trailer on the left holds your money (Donnie tells you): go in, beat the enemy and take the cash. It is optional and missable, but it adds to your “professional” reputation.

5. Take off. Shoot your way to the seaplane, or run to it and heal with Zombix if needed.

6. Back to the marina. Fly to the pink marker, land on the water by slowing down and drive to the marina. A cutscene introduces Brian, Jason’s boss, and the mission ends.

Tips

  • The binoculars are also used to study animals: you need to study 100 of 255 for 100%, and progress during the story already counts.
  • Zombix is the healing item (up to 5; sold at convenience stores).
  • Reputation: shooting limbs incapacitates without killing (“hardened”); full stealth gives “professional”; killing gives “psychopath”, and killing police or civilians can reach “irredeemable” (police may open fire on sight).
