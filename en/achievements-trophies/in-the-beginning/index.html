Official

How to get it

When: Any time.

  1. Go to the Ocean View Rooftop Bar in Ocean Beach.
  2. Buy an Apple Martini and drink it standing next to the bar.

Miscellaneous trophies

  • Master of None (bronze): Try each of the nine activities once: fishing, hunting, golf, shooting range, pool, sinking a basketball shot, an arcade game, winning a race and doing a stunt.
  • Earn Cash, Not Kudos (bronze): Win 20 different races out of the 40 on the map.
  • Stick Fiddler (bronze): Finish an arcade game. Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III and Time Jackers Rewind are on the TV at home from Chapter 1; Bang For Your Buck is on arcade cabinets around the map.
  • Gunslinger (bronze): Take down 5 enemies in a single Combat Focus (L3 + R3 while aiming).
  • Personal Best (bronze): Max out both Jason's and Lucia's strength (check under Touchpad › Jason & Lucia › Strength).
  • Actions Speak Louder (bronze): Do 500 actions that change your criminal profile; the count shows when you select the trophy.
  • Bad Luck, I Guess (bronze): How to unlock it isn't confirmed yet.

On PS5 and Xbox

On PS5 it is a bronze trophy; on Xbox Series X|S it is a 5G achievement. It unlocks exactly the same way on both consoles and every trophy is single-player.
