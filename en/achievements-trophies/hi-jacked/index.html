Complete VCIA Takedown.

Official

How to get it

When: Chapter 4.

  1. It's a story trophy: it pops automatically at the Chapter 4 mission VCIA Takedown.
  2. It can't be missed: follow the main story and you'll get it.

Useful pages

Mission walkthroughView page

Story trophies

  • Just Like Paradise (bronze): It's a story trophy: it pops automatically at the end of Gone Fishing, the first Chapter 1 mission.
  • Paradise Lost (bronze): It's a story trophy: it pops automatically at the first Chapter 2 mission, What's in the Bag?.
  • T**s Ass Drug Money (bronze): It's a story trophy: it pops automatically at the first Chapter 3 mission, Welcome to Vice City.
  • Vice City, Baby! (bronze): It's a story trophy: it pops automatically at the first two Chapter 4 missions, Ocean Beach and Another Life.
  • Top Of The World, Ma (bronze): It's a story trophy: it pops automatically at the last Chapter 4 mission, Starting From Zero.
  • You Gotta Know When to Get Off (gold): It's a story trophy: it pops automatically at the end of the final mission, Starting From Zero.
  • Trust (bronze): It's a story trophy: it pops automatically at the Chapter 2 mission Breakfast Run.

On PS5 and Xbox

On PS5 it is a bronze trophy; on Xbox Series X|S it is a 25G achievement. It unlocks exactly the same way on both consoles and every trophy is single-player.
