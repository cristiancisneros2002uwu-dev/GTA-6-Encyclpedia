GTA 6 achievements FAQ

How many achievements does GTA 6 have?

55 in total: 54 achievements worth 1,000G on Xbox Series X|S and, on PS5, 54 trophies plus the platinum. The list is the same on both consoles.

What's the hardest achievement?

A Long Road (100% completion). The Shootist (every shooting range gold) and Cunning Stunts (250 stunts) also take a while.

Are there missable achievements?

Possibly one: Positive Mental Attitude, because of the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house. Everything else can be done after the story.

How long does it take to get them all?

Around 200 hours. See the platinum guide for the recommended order.

Do cheats block achievements?

Yes: while a cheat is on, achievements don't unlock and you can't save in that session.

Are there online achievements?

No. They're all story mode.
