Rob a jewelry store without being detected.

Community

How to get it

  1. Rob a jewelry store without being detected.
  2. You need the Snap Gun from Yasmani’s jobs (Chapter 3).
  3. Go in through the back door; the safe code is in the back room.

On PS5 and Xbox

On PS5 it is a bronze trophy; on Xbox Series X|S it is a 10G achievement. It unlocks exactly the same way on both consoles and every trophy is single-player.
