Rob Kingpin Bowling.

Official

How to get it

When: Chapter 2.

  1. Do the Chapter 2 optional mission Hit Kingpin Bowling in Port Gellhorn.
  2. Buy sticky bombs at Ammu-Nation and steal three things: the luxury watch in the southeast office, the Gutterball Moreland 850 shotgun on the northeast table and the northeast hallway safe.
  3. It pops after you lose the cops. If you missed something, the robbery respawns later.

Crime and heist trophies

  • Hot Together (bronze): With Jason and Lucia together, lose a 4-star wanted level and get out of the red zone.
  • Retail Therapy (bronze): With your partner, rob three shops back to back without losing your wanted level.
  • Sneakthief (bronze): Finish Yasmani's jewelry jobs to unlock the Snap Gun and jewelry robberies.
  • Maximum Withdrawal (silver): First finish the Chapter 4 optional mission That's Where The Money Is.
  • A Classic Blitz Play (bronze): It pops on its own in the Chapter 4 optional mission That's Where The Money Is, since a large vault robbery counts.
  • Pest Control (bronze): Rob one illegal operation from each of the 9 gangs (there are 39 in total).
  • Dime on the Dollar (bronze): The Chapter 3 mission Credit Check unlocks the Dye Pack Jammer.

On PS5 and Xbox

On PS5 it is a bronze trophy; on Xbox Series X|S it is a 5G achievement. It unlocks exactly the same way on both consoles and every trophy is single-player.
