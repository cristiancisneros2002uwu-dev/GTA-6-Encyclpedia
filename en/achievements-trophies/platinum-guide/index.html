Vice Got a Price!

Platinum / 0G. Earn every other trophy.

How to get it
Official

Recommended roadmap

  1. Play the story (60–80 h). Scan vehicles, study animals and rob shops as you go.
  2. Do the meditation at Jason's house in Chapter 1: it is the only missable (Positive Mental Attitude).
  3. Save manually at the start of every chapter and before the final mission: there is no mission replay.
  4. After the credits, finish the 100% checklist (about 200 h total): robberies, 8 vaults, 39 operations, underwater finds, shooting ranges, drones and Bret.
  5. Finish with Strangers & Freaks (8 strands), the questlines with their own trophies (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret) and the misc trophies.
  6. Do not use cheats on your main save: they disable trophies and saving.

Key facts

  • DifficultyMedium; the 100% is the long part
  • Estimated timeAbout 200 hours
  • Missable trophies1 (Positive Mental Attitude)
  • Online trophies0
