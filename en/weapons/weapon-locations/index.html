Where to get weapons in GTA 6

All 24 Ammu-Nation stores and what each store type sells.

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

How it works

Buy weapons at Ammu-Nation or pick them up from enemies. Hover over the orange map icon to see the store type: Limited, Standard, Hunting or Expanded.

Only the 6 Expanded stores sell sniper rifles, grenade launchers, rocket launchers and their ammo. Each Ammu-Nation also sells a unique variant.
