How it compares

The Deever Tech GLR40 is one of 3 grenade and rocket launchers with known stats in GTA 6. Its damage (9.2/10) is right at the category average (9.3/10). It also has the best magazine in its category and has the best accuracy in its category.

Where to get it

  • $15,500 (Expanded)
  • Firearms are bought, upgraded and restocked at Ammu-Nation; some unlock as you progress through the story.
  • For the Gunslinger trophy, use a weapon with a big magazine and good accuracy during Combat Focus.
