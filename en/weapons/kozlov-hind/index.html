How it compares

The Kozlov Hind is one of 6 assault rifles with known stats in GTA 6. Its damage (5.4/10) is right at the category average (5.3/10). It also has the best magazine in its category, has the lowest accuracy in its category, has the best handling in its category and has the lowest range in its category.
