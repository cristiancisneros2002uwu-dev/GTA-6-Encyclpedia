About GTA 6 and this encyclopedia

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar states that the digital release occurs at midnight local time on November 19 in each region. The game is set in the fictional state of Leonida, including Vice City, and follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. This independent encyclopedia gathers verified information and distinguishes official facts from visual observations and community findings.
