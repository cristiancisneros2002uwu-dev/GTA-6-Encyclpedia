About GTA 6 and this encyclopedia

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) launches on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It is set in the fictional state of Leonida, which includes Vice City, and its protagonists are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. This independent encyclopedia covers characters, the map, vehicles, weapons, missions and cheats, and labels every fact as Official, Visual, Seen in demos, Community or Unconfirmed.
