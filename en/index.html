About GTA 6 and this encyclopedia

Grand Theft Auto VI is out now on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S since November 19, 2026; it unlocked at local midnight in each region. The game is set in the fictional state of Leonida, including Vice City, and follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. This independent encyclopedia gathers verified information and distinguishes official facts from visual observations and community findings.

New · updated guides

We filled the easter eggs section with 36 secrets and how to find them, including 6 hidden cars and money stashes, and added new guides: how to turn on headlights and high beams, how to lose the cops and how to win over Lucia. The radio now lists over 800 songs, including the secret The Vault Radio station, and the money guide adds the best Chapter 1 methods.
