News editorial standards

We prioritize official announcements, patch notes and changes that can be checked in-game. Each news item will include its date, primary source, affected platforms and a player-focused explanation.

Rumors and leaks will never be headlined as confirmed facts; if mentioned, they will be clearly labeled unverified.
