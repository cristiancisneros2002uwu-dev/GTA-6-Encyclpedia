Zoomable GTA 6 interactive map with filters: easter eggs, hidden cars, money, strip clubs, Ammu-Nation, shops and over 720 locations in Leonida.

How to use the map

Tap the map to activate it, then drag with one finger and zoom with two (or with your mouse wheel). The ☰ Filters button turns each category on or off, and the search box finds any place by name, such as Ammu-Nation, Dexter or NINE1NINE. Tap a pin to see what is there and open its entry in the encyclopedia. Numbers group nearby places together: they split apart as you zoom in.
