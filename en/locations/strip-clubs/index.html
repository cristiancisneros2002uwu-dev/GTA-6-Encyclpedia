Strip club locations 👠

Every strip club and nightclub in Leonida, and where to find their dancers' cards.

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Where every GTA 6 strip club (the high-heel icon) and nightclub is, plus where to find each club's dancer cards.

Quick answer

On the in-game map, strip clubs are marked with a high-heel icon 👠 (Jack of Hearts uses a crowned heart). So far every guide confirms 3 strip clubs: NINE1NINE and Jack of Hearts in Vice City and Delights Cabaret in Port Gellhorn. There are also 3 nightclubs and 90 dancer cards hidden around Leonida.

How to find them on the in-game map

  • As you clear the map fog, clubs get marked automatically with a blue icon.
  • To filter them: Map › Activities (dumbbell icon) › Clubs. It shows how many you've found.
  • Walk in with weapons away and hold L2 (or LT) while looking at a dancer to greet her.
  • On our interactive map they're under the "Strip clubs (high heel)" filter.
