Banks in GTA 6: quick answers

How many banks are there in GTA 6?

GTA 6 has 14 small banks (cash drawer and safe) and 8 large banks with a vault, from Fleeca, Lombank, Shorefront and Hobbs Milford. Most are in Vice City and Vice Beach. They unlock with Credit Check in Chapter 3.

Where are all the banks in GTA 6?

Here is the full list: Lombank – Bayside; Fleeca – Downtown; Lombank – Downtown; Shorefront – Downtown; Fleeca – Ocean Beach; Hobbs Milford – Ocean Beach; Lombank – Ocean Beach; Shorefront – Ocean Beach; Fleeca – Peacock Bay; Lombank – Peacock Bay; Shorefront – Rockridge; Fleeca – Washington Beach; Hobbs Milford – SW 4th Street; Fleeca – Tequesta; Lombank – Tequesta; Fleeca – VCIA; Fleeca – Key Lento; Shorefront – Watson Bay; Shorefront – Ambrosia; Druthers State Bank; Gibson Brothers Bank. Each one links to the interactive map.
