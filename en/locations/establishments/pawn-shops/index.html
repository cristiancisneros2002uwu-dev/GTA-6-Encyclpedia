Pawn shops in GTA 6: quick answers

How many pawn shops are there in GTA 6?

There are 19 pawn shops in GTA 6, 13 of them in Vice-Dale County. All count as Smash 'n' Grabs, and some, like Pawn & Gun, also sell guns.

Where are all the pawn shops in GTA 6?

Here is the full list: Pawn & Gun – Crosstown; Vice City Pawn; Hamlet Pawn; Pawn Stash; Zenon's Pawn Shop; Fast Cash; Casa de Empeño; Dorado Pawn Shop; Down Town Pawn; White Sands Jewelry Pawn; Pawn City; Ernesto's Pawn; Joyeria Empenos; Timeless Peculiars; Avery Pawn; Buck's Jewelry & Pawn; Pawn & Gun – Port Gellhorn; Florence & Co.; Vice City Gun & Pawn. Each one links to the interactive map.

Yes. Every location on this page has a link to the interactive map, and the list is sorted by county and neighborhood.
