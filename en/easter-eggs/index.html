GTA 6 easter eggs and how to find them

36 secrets, references and hidden encounters in Leonida, with their location and the steps to trigger them.

Every GTA 6 easter egg the community has found so far: the tower egg, Bigfoot, the UFO, Dexter and more, with where they are and how to trigger them.

These are the easter eggs players have found in Leonida since launch. Each one lists where it is, what to do and what happens. Rockstar doesn't publish an official list, so we'll keep adding more as they turn up.

Mysteries and creatures (6)

A literal Easter egg (radio tower)

Rockstar took the term literally: at the top of the tallest radio tower, the one visible since the first trailer, there is a giant nest full of painted eggs.

Where: Southern Vice-Dale County, west of Southside and Peacock Bay and north of Hamlet.

How: Reach the base of the tower and climb the ladders and platforms. The climb takes 6 to 7 minutes. At the top, pick up one of the eggs.

What happens: Keep it or throw it off the edge: throwing it plays a short scene where the egg smashes on the windshield of a real estate agent arguing on the phone. You can also BASE jump from the same platform.

Community

Bigfoot: the swamp Skunk Ape

GTA 6's Bigfoot is the Skunk Ape, Florida's take on the legend: a tall, hairy creature wrapped in a green haze. A Weazel News report on the TV in Jason's house already mentions it.

Where: Grassrivers, mostly on the road along the east side of Cottonmouth Lake toward Gambit Bay (there are also sightings in the swamps of southern Mariana County).

How: Listen for a loud roar and follow the trail with your tracking ability. It leads to a human-shaped tree (its effigy). Investigate it.

What happens: The smell knocks your character out and you catch a glimpse of the creature. It can't be hurt or caught. A 'Have You Seen Skunk Ape???' poster leads to Kirk, a cryptid hunter with a side mission line to track it down.

UFO abduction and hidden trophy

One of the most searched-for launch secrets: a full abduction that also unlocks a hidden trophy.

Where: Dirt road near the Starlet Motel, east of Port Gellhorn (Kelly County).

How: After Chapter 1, go at night (ideally after 2:00 a.m. in-game). Look for a roadside car with flickering headlights, an open trunk and clothes scattered around. Follow the blue glow into the trees and go back to the car when the woman asks you to.

What happens: You black out and wake up three days later. It unlocks the hidden bronze trophy 'The Truth Is Out There'.

Community

The Pelican Valley crab lab

An abandoned research outpost where failed crab experiments took place.

Where: Southwest of the Pelican Valley swamps, just north of the Keys.

How: Go inside the lab and solve the puzzle that opens the safe.

What happens: Just over $1,000 and the Crabshack VF5 weapon. Some players also report a rare scene with a giant crab; what triggers it is still unclear.

Community

The Belville UFO house

A house packed with UFO and conspiracy nods. A photo of this very house was already hanging in the Lil' Probe Inn bar in GTA San Andreas: The Definitive Edition, the only one on that wall that didn't match a known location.

Where: Belville, Leonard County.

How: Check the car's trunk and the house's garage.

What happens: There's a snake in the trunk, a conspiracy den in the garage and a meteorite you can sell online.

Community

Mysteries the community is still working on

Three leads that point to a Mount Chiliad-style mystery and still have no clear answer.

Where: An abandoned research facility that opens with code 2314 on its keypad (its exact location isn't confirmed yet); standing stones and a meteor crater on top of Mount Kalaga; and the Monument of Leonidan Supremacy, north of Port Gellhorn.

How: If you find the keypad, enter 2314. For the rest, explore the top of Mount Kalaga and the area around the monument.

What happens: So far we only know the code opens the facility. We'll update this as soon as there's more.

Hidden cars (5)

Classic Pfister Comet at a luxury mansion

A magenta retro Comet with green rims, parked at the driveway of a mansion in Vice City's wealthy area.

Where: Vice City's luxury residential islands, in the driveway of a mansion with an enterable interior (usually empty at night).

How: Walk onto the property. The keys usually aren't there, so you'll have to steal it; since it's an old car, you can break in with no trouble.

What happens: It's yours to drive. Note: it has an advanced tracker, so it's good for a joyride but not for selling or keeping without the police finding it.

Imponte Ruiner Turbo with nitrous, next to Jason's house

A black muscle car with an exposed engine and factory nitrous, hidden right next to where you start.

Where: Next to Jason's house, in Mariana County (the Keys).

How: Find the Ruiner Turbo parked at the neighboring house. It's locked and has an alarm, so break in and get away quickly.

What happens: The best part: it has no tracker, so you can keep it. The nitrous is strong; brake before corners.

Vehicle page: See vehicle page

Community

Classic Vapid roadster on the gardeners' island

A 1930s teal roadster kept inside the garage of a mansion on a private island.

Where: A private island in northern Vice-Dale County, near the Leonard County line. The car is inside the mansion's garage.

How: Go in through the back, from the waterfront garden: the front has security and gardeners who will give you away.

What happens: It's unlocked with no tracker and no alarm, so you can drive off with no trouble.

Community

Vapid Hustler (1932): the flame hot rod

A black 1932 hot rod with pink flames and an exposed engine, kept in a house garage in Vice City.

Where: A house garage in Vice-Dale County (Vice City).

How: Go into the garage at night. The catch: two cars are parked in the driveway blocking the exit, so you'll need to squeeze it out or move one.

What happens: It has an alarm and a standard tracker: use it for a ride, or deal with the tracker before trying to keep it.

Vehicle page: See vehicle page

Community

The Scooby-Doo van (Bravado Youga XL 1979)

A green and yellow 1979 Youga XL that looks just like Scooby-Doo's Mystery Machine.

Where: An alley in downtown Vice City, in Vice-Dale County.

How: Head to the alley and get in. It doesn't always spawn: if it isn't there, come back at another time of day.

What happens: It has no security and no tracker, so you can keep it.

Hidden money and weapons (3)

The criminals' warehouse: over $10,000 early on

One of the best early-game hauls: an industrial warehouse full of criminals with cash, drugs and an exclusive collectible card.

Where: South Vice-Dale County, a little east of Hamlet. It's a lot with a warehouse, trucks and vans.

How: Take out the criminals and loot them. On a table there's a bag with over $6,000; grab it. Then search everything: there's a safe inside the building, and the vans have loot too.

What happens: About $10,000 in total, plus drugs and a very exclusive collectible card. Load it into your car and take it home to stash it before anything happens.

Community

Collectible cards worth up to $2,700

The collectible cards you find don't just complete sets: you can sell them, and some are worth a lot.

Where: Pawn shops, such as Vice City Pawn.

How: Go into the pawn shop, open the collectible cards tab, pick the set (for example, Los Santos Pounders) and sell.

What happens: Cards worth $2,700 have turned up. Selling doesn't remove your collection progress, so sell them as you find them.

Free weapons from parked police cars

Police cars carry weapons in the trunk, and you can keep them.

Where: Any parked police car with no officers around; the more secluded the better (a parking lot works well).

How: Put on a mask, cap and sunglasses. Park your car behind it, pick the trunk lock (the alarm goes off), grab the weapons and run. Stow them in your car.

What happens: You can get two long guns, a handgun, body armor or any mix. If police are nearby, they'll spot you right away.

Encounters and hidden characters (8)

Cool Dog and Dog Island

If you get stranded at sea, a dog wearing sunglasses shows up on a jet ski to rescue you.

Where: Open sea. Gambit Bay is the easiest spot.

How: Take the boat from the dock at Jason's house, head into Gambit Bay, jump in and swim away from the shore. After about 5 minutes alone you'll hear barking.

What happens: Cool Dog picks you up and takes you to an island full of dogs. Throw him the ball and follow him around the island to a stash worth about $1,200.

Community

The talking parrot at Three Sheets Liquor

An early-game secret that pays well and can be missed if you progress too far.

Where: Back room of the Three Sheets Liquor store.

How: Do Shirley's rum errand. Then come back, jump the side fence (don't go behind the counter), beat the thugs and find the caged red parrot. Check the grate in the wall next to the generator.

What happens: A stash with more than $1,300. Do it early: the errand is missable.

Community

The cat in the stolen car

Some parked cars have a cat inside.

Where: Any parked car with a cat inside.

How: Steal the car and drive off.

What happens: The cat attacks the driver in a short scene and your character ends up tossing it out the window.

Community

Camilo and the 'Trunk Challenge'

A streamer who climbed into a car trunk for a challenge.

Where: Inside the trunk of a stolen car, while playing as Lucia.

How: Open the trunk of a car you just stole.

What happens: Lucia finds Camilo doing the 'Trunk Challenge', and he keeps talking to his chat throughout the chase.

Community

The furry rescue

Leonida has a 'furry ban' that's satirized in TV ads and in the Vice City Horn newspaper. On the street, you can run into the consequences.

Where: By the roadside at night, while playing as Lucia.

How: When you see police chasing a man in a fox costume, decide what to do.

What happens: Let the cops take him, or break the blockade, free him and help the furries escape.

Community

The lawsuit witness

An absurd random event on the streets of Vice City.

Where: Vice City streets.

How: Stay close when a truck rear-ends a sedan.

What happens: The driver deliberately sets his own legs on fire and asks you to be a witness in his lawsuit against the truck company.

Community

William Tell target practice

An encounter in the middle of nowhere.

Where: Rural areas; you notice it by the gunfire in the distance.

How: Go toward the sound of gunfire.

What happens: Two men invite you to shoot objects one of them holds above his head. Aim carefully: hitting his hand changes how it ends.

Community

The dumpster lawyer

A man who lives in a dumpster and gives legal advice.

Where: Leonida streets, next to a dumpster.

How: Talk to him when you find him.

What happens: He offers legal advice and you choose whether to pay him in pills.

References to other games and shows (8)

Dexter Morgan's apartment

The building where Dexter lived in the TV series is recreated in Vice City, along with his boat.

Where: Washington Island, in eastern Vice City, between the Pay 'n' Spray and Vice Beach Top Fun Drones icons. Look for a small light-blue building with a cylindrical staircase on its south side.

How: Go up to the second floor and head to the corner unit at the end of the walkway.

What happens: The door is locked, but the 'Slice of Life' boat is at the dock next door. If you dive nearby, there are trash bags on the sea floor just like in the show.

Community

Paul Walker's green Eclipse

A Fast & Furious tribute: the green Maibatsu Penumbra FF, based on the Mitsubishi Eclipse from the movie.

Where: A villa garage on Vespucci Island, near Vice Beach.

How: Get into the villa, break through to the garage, smash the car window and hotwire it.

What happens: The car is yours. Open the trunk: there's a snake waiting for you.

Community

The boat with a Los Santos GPS

A GTA V nod right at home.

Where: The boat in the yard of Jason's house.

How: Check the boat's GPS screen.

What happens: It shows the map of Los Santos.

Community

Michael's ringtone

You can use the same ringtone Michael De Santa had in GTA V.

Where: Phone › Settings › Ringtone.

How: Pick the 'Wind Chimes' ringtone.

What happens: Your phone rings just like Michael's.

Community

The New Austin cowboy novel

A reminder that Red Dead Redemption and GTA share a universe.

Where: Several places; one copy is in Jacob's Tavern, in Millport.

How: Read the Western novel you find.

What happens: It tells the story of a cowboy in New Austin, the Red Dead Redemption region. Cars with New Austin license plates have also been spotted.

Community

A Liberty City mobster's memoir

A book that ties into the story of GTA IV.

Where: Jason's house, on the small table by the back door (Chapter 1).

How: Read the book.

What happens: It's the memoir of a former Liberty City mobster who mentions events from 2008, the year GTA IV takes place.

More nods to GTA V, GTA IV and Mount Chiliad

Small details spread across Leonida for long-time fans.

Where: All over the map.

How: Look at magazines, podcasts and decor.

What happens: A magazine featuring yoga teacher Fabien LaRouche, a true crime podcast about Floyd and Debra, lawn gnomes, desert postcards, Mr. Raspberry Jam and a truck covered in conspiracy theories with the eye from the Mount Chiliad mural.

Community

The Cristiano Ronaldo statue at 'Messi's house'

A player found a gold statue mimicking Cristiano Ronaldo's celebration at a bayfront mansion facing the Vice City skyline. Since Messi lives in Miami, the community dubbed it 'the CR7 statue in Messi's house'.

Where: A mansion with a pool and dock on the Vice City bay. The exact location isn't confirmed yet.

How: Search the waterfront mansions with a direct view of downtown Vice City.

What happens: It's just a visual nod: the statue stands by the pool.

Fun details (6)

Painting the bedroom for Lucia

You can pick colors and paint the walls by hand with a roller.

Where: Jason's house.

How: Choose a color and paint the walls.

What happens: It boosts your relationship with Lucia. See the Jason and Lucia relationship guide for more ways to do it.

Community

Feeding the pet snake

Jason keeps a snake at home.

Where: Jason's house.

How: Feed it.

What happens: The snake catches and swallows the mouse with a very realistic animation.

Community

Petting dogs and cats

You can pet the dogs and bodega cats you come across.

Where: Anywhere with dogs or cats.

How: Walk up and interact.

What happens: Careful: sometimes a car door swings open into the dog you're petting.

Community

JoySystem console and retro arcade

There are video games inside the video game.

Where: Your living room (JoySystem console) and a retro video game store.

How: Use the console on the TV or walk into the store.

What happens: The console has mini-games. At the retro store you can buy headphones to listen to music on foot and play a Big Buck Hunter-style hunting arcade game.

Community

No socks with flip-flops

A joke hidden in the clothing menu.

Where: Clothing menu.

How: Put flip-flops on Jason and try to pick socks.

What happens: The socks option shows a padlock.

Community

Kilometers instead of miles (and a 24-hour clock)

The game defaults to miles and pounds, but you can switch to metric.

Where: Settings › User Interface (at the very bottom).

How: Under Units of Measurement choose Metric. In the same menu you can switch weight to kilograms, temperature to Celsius and the clock to 24-hour format.

What happens: The minimap, speedometer and distances show in kilometers.
