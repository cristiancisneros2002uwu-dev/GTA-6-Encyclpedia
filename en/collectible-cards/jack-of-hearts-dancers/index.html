Jack of Hearts Dancers cards

Jack of Hearts dancers set · 36 cards · 13 located so far

Confirmation levelOfficialVisualSeen in demosCommunityUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Where to find all 36 Jack of Hearts Dancers collectible cards in GTA 6: 13 located with exact spots, sell prices and tips to complete the set.

Quick answer

  • 36 cards in total; this guide has 13 located and 23 still to be documented.
  • Colour versions found: deep red, gold, pink, purple, red, silver.
  • Most found in: Mariana County (Leonida Keys) (6), Kelly County (3), Vice-Dale County (3).
  • Completing 3 sets earns Don't Touch My Deck; 100% needs 15 of the 18 sets.

23 cards in this set are still unlocated. The game came out on November 19 and players are still finding them; this page is updated as they're confirmed. Meanwhile, yellow containers around the map can drop random cards that do count toward your set.

Tips to complete Jack of Hearts Dancers

  • Dancers aren't only at their club: they turn up in motels, trailers, apartments and rooftops all over the map.
  • Many come in colour versions (pink, red, purple, silver, gold). Each colour counts as a separate card.
  • Watch for missables: Vicki (Delights) is at the Starlet Motel and can't be picked up after Chapter 2.
  • To track progress: Map › Progress › Collections › Collectible Cards.
  • For the club itself, see the strip club locations.

Where to sell these cards

Pawn shops buy them (they open in Chapter 2). Selling doesn't remove a card from your collection, so it pays to sell duplicates. Full pawn shop list and prices in the main cards guide.
