Ed Comics Universe cards

Comic set · 36 cards · 6 located so far

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Where to find all 36 Ed Comics Universe collectible cards in GTA 6: 6 located with exact spots, sell prices and tips to complete the set.

Quick answer

  • 36 cards in total; this guide has 6 located and 30 still to be documented.
  • Colour versions found: blue, bronze, orange, pink, silver.
  • Most found in: Kelly County (3), Vice-Dale County (3).
  • Completing 3 sets earns Don't Touch My Deck; 100% needs 15 of the 18 sets.

30 cards in this set are still unlocated. The game came out on November 19 and players are still finding them; this page is updated as they're confirmed. Meanwhile, yellow containers around the map can drop random cards that do count toward your set.

Tips to complete Ed Comics Universe

  • Comic cards show up a lot in trailers, warehouses, unfinished buildings and watchtowers.
  • Each character usually comes in several colours (blue, purple, red, silver, bronze...) and each one counts separately, which is why these sets have 36 cards.
  • Use focus mode when you walk into a trailer or warehouse: the yellow card icon tells you if one is nearby.
  • To track progress: Map › Progress › Collections › Collectible Cards.

Where to sell these cards

Pawn shops buy them (they open in Chapter 2). Selling doesn't remove a card from your collection, so it pays to sell duplicates. Full pawn shop list and prices in the main cards guide.
