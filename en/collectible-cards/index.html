GTA 6 collectible cards

All 18 sets and 480 cards: where to find them, what they're worth and where to sell them.

Confirmation levelOfficialVisualSeen in demosCommunityUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

GTA 6 collectible cards guide: all 18 sets, where to find each card, how much they sell for and which pawn shops buy them.

Quick answer

There are 480 cards across 18 sets. You can't buy them: they sit in fixed spots around the map. Pawn shops pay from $20 to over $1,000, and the rarest go past $2,000: the silver Ulysses Whitlock pays $2,600 and the gold Leroy Rook about $6,000. Selling a card doesn't remove it from your collection.

How to find cards

  • Indoors they're usually on a surface (tables, shelves, fridges) or stuck to a wall.
  • Outdoors they're usually in yellow phone boxes on walls or in tool boxes.
  • Use focus mode: a yellow card icon shows up when one is within reach.
  • Yellow containers around the map can drop random cards; they count toward your collection even though guides don't list them.
  • Some cards come in colour versions (bronze, silver, gold, red, blue, purple...). Each colour counts as a separate card.
  • There are duplicates: they don't add to the set, but you can sell them.
  • Check safes and lockboxes: many have the code written nearby (on a wall or a note). The bronze Laki Maivia is in a safe with code 44017.
  • Watch out for missables: Victor Maravilla and Vicki are at the Starlet Motel and can't be picked up after Chapter 2.
  • To track progress: Map › Progress › Collections › Collectible Cards.

Where to sell your cards

Pawn shops buy them. They open in Chapter 2 after the Coffee Break mission; the Hamlet one is the closest at that point. Not every shop buys everything: Vice City Pawn takes cards, doubloons and meteorites, and Pawn Stash takes cards only. On the map, open Exploration › Collectors to see which ones buy them. You can sell a whole set at once, and the card stays recorded in your collection.
