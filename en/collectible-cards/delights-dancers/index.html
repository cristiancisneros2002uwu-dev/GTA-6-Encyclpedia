Delights Dancers cards

Delights dancers set · 18 cards · 9 located so far

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Where to find all 18 Delights Dancers collectible cards in GTA 6: 9 located with exact spots, sell prices and tips to complete the set.

Quick answer

  • 18 cards in total; this guide has 9 located and 9 still to be documented.
  • Most found in: Kelly County (3), Vice-Dale County (2), Mariana County (Leonida Keys) (2).
  • Completing 3 sets earns Don't Touch My Deck; 100% needs 15 of the 18 sets.

Tips to complete Delights Dancers

  • Dancers aren't only at their club: they turn up in motels, trailers, apartments and rooftops all over the map.
  • Many come in colour versions (pink, red, purple, silver, gold). Each colour counts as a separate card.
  • Watch for missables: Vicki (Delights) is at the Starlet Motel and can't be picked up after Chapter 2.
  • To track progress: Map › Progress › Collections › Collectible Cards.
  • For the club itself, see the strip club locations.

Where to sell these cards

Pawn shops buy them (they open in Chapter 2). Selling doesn't remove a card from your collection, so it pays to sell duplicates. Full pawn shop list and prices in the main cards guide.
