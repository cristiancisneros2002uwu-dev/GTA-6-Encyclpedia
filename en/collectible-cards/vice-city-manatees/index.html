Vice City Manatees cards

American football set · 24 cards · 7 located so far

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Where to find all 24 Vice City Manatees collectible cards in GTA 6: 7 located with exact spots, sell prices and tips to complete the set.

Quick answer

  • 24 cards in total; this guide has 7 located and 17 still to be documented.
  • Colour versions found: gold, green, pink, silver.
  • Most found in: Vice-Dale County (5), Leonard County (1), Kelly County (1).
  • Completing 3 sets earns Don't Touch My Deck; 100% needs 15 of the 18 sets.

17 cards in this set are still unlocated. The game came out on November 19 and players are still finding them; this page is updated as they're confirmed. Meanwhile, yellow containers around the map can drop random cards that do count toward your set.

Tips to complete Vice City Manatees

  • Sports cards turn up a lot in garages, gyms and stadiums: check shelves, workbenches and benches.
  • Look in locker rooms and dugouts at ballparks and courts, and in gym offices.
  • Several players come in colour versions (bronze, silver, gold or another colour). Each version counts separately toward the set.
  • To track progress: Map › Progress › Collections › Collectible Cards.

Where to sell these cards

Pawn shops buy them (they open in Chapter 2). Selling doesn't remove a card from your collection, so it pays to sell duplicates. Full pawn shop list and prices in the main cards guide.
