Backyard Wrestling cards

Wrestling set · 12 cards · 6 located so far

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Where to find all 12 Backyard Wrestling collectible cards in GTA 6: 6 located with exact spots, sell prices and tips to complete the set.

Quick answer

  • 12 cards in total; this guide has 6 located and 6 still to be documented.
  • Most found in: Kelly County (2), Leonard County (2), Vice-Dale County (1).
  • Completing 3 sets earns Don't Touch My Deck; 100% needs 15 of the 18 sets.

6 cards in this set are still unlocated. The game came out on November 19 and players are still finding them; this page is updated as they're confirmed. Meanwhile, yellow containers around the map can drop random cards that do count toward your set.

Tips to complete Backyard Wrestling

  • This is the shortest set in the game (12 cards) and the quickest one to finish for the Don't Touch My Deck trophy.
  • Many sit in trailers, treehouses and back yards, fitting the backyard-wrestling theme.
  • There's at least one duplicate (Chico Bonez shows up in two places): the second one doesn't count, but you can sell it.
  • To track progress: Map › Progress › Collections › Collectible Cards.

Where to sell these cards

Pawn shops buy them (they open in Chapter 2). Selling doesn't remove a card from your collection, so it pays to sell duplicates. Full pawn shop list and prices in the main cards guide.
