America's Next Top Hooker cards

Novelty (TV show) set · 24 cards · 10 located so far

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Where to find all 24 America's Next Top Hooker collectible cards in GTA 6: 10 located with exact spots, sell prices and tips to complete the set.

Quick answer

  • 24 cards in total; this guide has 10 located and 14 still to be documented.
  • Colour versions found: bronze, gold, silver.
  • Most found in: Vice-Dale County (3), Mariana County (Leonida Keys) (2), Lummox County (Mount Kalaga) (2).
  • Completing 3 sets earns Don't Touch My Deck; 100% needs 15 of the 18 sets.

14 cards in this set are still unlocated. The game came out on November 19 and players are still finding them; this page is updated as they're confirmed. Meanwhile, yellow containers around the map can drop random cards that do count toward your set.

Tips to complete America's Next Top Hooker

  • Check public toilets, piers, tents and garages: several of these cards are in unglamorous spots.
  • Drake has three versions (bronze, silver and gold), each in a different region of the map.
  • Some sit on small islands in the Leonida Keys and the Sandbars: bring a boat or jet ski.
  • To track progress: Map › Progress › Collections › Collectible Cards.

Where to sell these cards

Pawn shops buy them (they open in Chapter 2). Selling doesn't remove a card from your collection, so it pays to sell duplicates. Full pawn shop list and prices in the main cards guide.
