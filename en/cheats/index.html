GTA 6 cheats

All 61 cheats, their codes and how to unlock them. Codes are the same on PS5 and Xbox.

Every GTA 6 cheat in one list: money, infinite ammo, vehicles and filters, with the D-pad code for PS5 and Xbox and the unlock requirement for each.

Quick answer

  • GTA 6 has 61 cheats; 19 codes are known so far and the rest are added as they're confirmed.
  • Entering the code isn't enough: each cheat is unlocked by meeting a requirement (most of them by finishing the story).
  • You enter them on the D-pad from the phone, so the code is the same on PS5 and Xbox.
  • The money cheat gives $250,000, but while cheats are on you can't save or earn trophies.
  • GTA 5 phone numbers don't work in GTA 6.

How to enter cheats

  1. Finish the story (or meet the cheat's requirement). Check the cheat list right after the credits: several will already be unlocked.
  2. Take out the phone with ↑ on the D-pad.
  3. On PS5 hold L1 and press R1; on Xbox Series X|S hold LB and press RB.
  4. Enter the code on the D-pad. If it worked, the Cheat list menu appears.
  5. Save first: while a cheat is active you can't save or earn trophies or achievements in that session.

FAQ

Is there a money cheat in GTA 6?

Yes: it gives $250,000 and unlocks when you finish the story (↑ → ↓ ← ↑ ← ↓ →). But since you can't save with cheats on, that money doesn't stay in your normal save. For real money, see the money guide.

Do cheats disable trophies?

Yes. While a cheat is on, trophies, achievements and saving are disabled for the rest of that session. Save first and use cheats in a separate session.

Why isn't a cheat working?

Most often it isn't unlocked yet: each cheat has its own requirement. Also check you took out the phone first and are holding L1/LB while pressing R1/RB.

Do GTA 5 cheats work?

No. Neither GTA 5 button codes nor phone numbers work in GTA 6.

Is there an invincibility cheat?

It isn't in the list of 61. The closest are the body armor and fall-damage immunity cheats.

Are they the same on PS5 and Xbox?

Yes. Every code uses only the D-pad; the only difference is the button to open the list (L1+R1 on PS5, LB+RB on Xbox).
