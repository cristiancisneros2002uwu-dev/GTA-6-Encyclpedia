Before you use a cheat

In GTA 6, cheats require meeting an in-game unlock requirement before they can be activated (for example, finishing the story or completing a number of challenges). Each entry lists its own.

We separate cheats with a confirmed D-pad combination from those whose combination is still pending.

Item names are translated from the Spanish game interface; official English names may differ.

How to enter cheats

Open the phone with ↑, hold L1 and press R1, then enter the sequence on the D-pad.

The cheats list appears after finishing the story. Activating a cheat temporarily disables trophies/achievements and saving, and progress from that session is lost when you quit. Save first and use a separate session.
