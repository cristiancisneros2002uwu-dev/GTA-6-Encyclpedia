Progression and 100% in GTA 6

How the characters improve, what unlocks in each chapter and everything the 100% checklist asks for.

Stats and profile

  • Health, Cardio and Strength: rise by working out at gyms, running, swimming and cycling. The Strength Regime challenge asks for Strength level 4 on both for 5 days in a row.
  • Focus: used by Combat Focus; some missions, like Get Shirley's Rum, improve it.
  • Criminal profile: professional, hardened, psychopath or irredeemable depending on how you act. 500 actions that change it earn the Actions Speak Louder trophy.
  • Jason–Lucia relationship: improves with errands, choices and visits (Take Care of Jason, Coming Home, Day Release).

Recommended route to 100%

  1. During the story: scan vehicles, study animals and rob stores as you go.
  2. Chapters 2 and 3: start Moses, Randy, Kenton and Bret as soon as they unlock.
  3. Chapter 4: do That's Where The Money Is… to open the vaults.
  4. After the credits: gang operations, banks, Payday Loans and Smash 'n' Grabs.
  5. Then activities: shooting ranges, races, drones, basketball, pool and mini golf.
  6. Finish with collectibles: cards, WAiNK, underwater discoveries, magazines, flamingos and styles.
  7. Expect about 200 hours for the full checklist.
