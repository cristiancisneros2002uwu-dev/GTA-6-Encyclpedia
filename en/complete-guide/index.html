Overview

Grand Theft Auto VI is by Rockstar Games. It is set in Leonida, a fictional state inspired by Florida, with Vice City as its main city. It launches on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Official

Single-player

It is described as a single-player experience at launch.

Official

30 FPS

Rockstar confirmed 30 FPS on all platforms, including PS5 Pro.

Official

Two protagonists

Jason and Lucia. Switching between them is instant when they are together and takes a few seconds when they are apart.

Official

Body and habits

Eating, working out and sleeping affect the protagonists’ physique and stats.

Official

Social media and audio

There is an in-game social network, radio and podcasts.

Official

Activities seen

Mini golf, dirt bike races, swimming, a zoo, shooting ranges, car trading, robberies and bank heists.
