GTA 6 controls and settings on Xbox Series X|S

On-foot controls, aiming settings, accessibility and sound, taken from the in-game menu.

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Kilometers instead of miles? Settings › User Interface › Units of Measurement › Metric. You can also switch weight to kilograms, temperature to Celsius and the clock to 24-hour there.

Looking for how to turn on headlights, use high beams or turn signals? It's in vehicle controls.

Confirmed in the controls menu

  • Waink app: D-pad up (shown as Phone in other previews).
  • Partner menu: D-pad down (also shown as Detonate explosives).
  • Focus: combat focus ability, set to LT + RT (2 options).
  • Fishing: the menu includes a control setting for fishing (Rotate, 3 options), which confirms the mechanic.
  • Nitro: the menu includes a control setting for vehicle nitro (Hold, 2 options).
