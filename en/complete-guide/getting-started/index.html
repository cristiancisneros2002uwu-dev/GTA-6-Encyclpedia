Getting started in GTA 6: step-by-step guide

What to do from minute one, how the game works and what not to miss on your first playthrough.

The essentials in 30 seconds

  • Story: a prologue and 5 chapters, 124 missions and about 60–80 hours. After the ending you stay in the open world.
  • Two protagonists: Jason and Lucia. At first the story decides who you control; from Coffee Break (Chapter 2) you can switch freely.
  • Map icons: pink icon with black letters = main mission; black icon with pink letters = optional; purple = side tasks and characters.
  • Saving: 15 manual slots and 1 autosave. There is no mission replay and no per-mission medals.
  • Money: cash in your pocket is lost when you die; deposit it at ATMs (BuckMe app).
  • Cheats: they only work after the story and they disable trophies and saving.

Step by step for your first playthrough

  1. Prologue (Lucia). Follow the prompts; the final arrest is mandatory. It teaches movement, robberies and getaways.
  2. Gone Fishing (Jason). Practice stealth and shoot arms and legs: that pushes your criminal profile toward “professional”. Grab the optional cash in the trailer.
  3. Buy your first long gun. In Gear Up pick the shotgun: it is the most useful early on and usually the only one you can afford.
  4. Open your BuckMe account. In Buck Me you get $100; from then on deposit your cash at ATMs often.
  5. Do the optional Coming Home errands. Groceries, flowers, the necklace, the haircut and Lucia’s clothes improve the relationship, and the necklace is used in Chapter 2.
  6. Meditate on Lucia’s terrace. After Down to Earth, come back in the morning and accept her invitation: it is the first meditation spot and it can be missed.
  7. Finish Chapter 1 optional content before Recalibration. Shirley, Kenton and Cal pause or become missable; save manually and keep at least $5,000 in savings, because afterwards you lose your cash and the house.
  8. Chapter 2 in Port Gellhorn. As Lucia, unlock Moses: selling untracked cars is the fastest early money.
  9. Raise $15,000 to move to Vice City. Thrillbillies, pawn shop robberies, Kingpin Bowling and Afternoon Delight are the best sources.
  10. From Chapter 3, mix story and open world. Scan vehicles with WAiNK, study animals, rob stores and start Randy, Kenton and Bret as soon as they appear: it all counts toward 100%.

Core systems worth understanding

Health and healing

Zombix is the healing item: you can carry up to 5 and it is sold at convenience stores. Eating, working out and sleeping also affect the characters’ stats.

Criminal profile

The way you act is tracked: stealth and limb shots move your profile toward “professional” or “hardened”; needless killing pushes it to “psychopath”, and killing police or civilians can leave it at “irredeemable”, with far more aggressive police.

Recognition and police

If the police are looking for you, leave the search area or hide crouched in dark spots. Respraying your car at a Pay 'n' Spray and changing clothes lowers how recognizable you are.

Phone and apps

  • WAiNK (D-pad up): scan vehicles, landmarks, curiosities and sculptures.
  • BuckMe: bank, savings and ATM Finder.
  • Police scanner: earned in Unwanted Attention.
  • Scooter Bros: register vehicles (after Moses).
  • Vice City Real Estate: buy garages (after Daily Driver).

Fences

Fences buy stolen goods: narcotics, jewelry, marked bills and cars. Brian is the first; later come Dunk, Moses, Randy, Yasmani and Tyrone. They show up with a green icon on the map.

Missable content

  • Shirley’s missions (Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter): only until Tourist Trap.
  • Donnie’s H&L .38 revolver: open the trunk before destroying the car in Don Gone.
  • Balcony meditation with Lucia: Chapter 1 only.
  • Dubious Provenance parts 2 and 3: if you don’t trust Halifax, they are lost at the end of Chapter 3.
  • Race Against Yago and This is a Free Call From…: they are time-limited (hourglass icon).
  • Cash and the Key Lento house: lost after Recalibration; the motel, after Checking Out.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Carrying lots of cash and dying: deposit it as soon as you can.
  • Not saving before a point of no return: there is no way to replay missions.
  • Selling Moses cars stolen near his garage: he rejects them.
  • Using cheats on your main save: they block trophies and saving.
  • Ignoring the open world until the end: scanning and studying as you go saves many hours for 100%.
