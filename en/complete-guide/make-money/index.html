How to make money in GTA 6

The best methods by stage of the game, how much they pay and how not to lose what you earn.

How money works

The loop is: steal → escape → sell to a fence → get cash → deposit → spend or save. Pocket cash is lost when you die; money in BuckMe is not.

  • Checking starts with a $15,000 limit that rises with your savings goals, up to $2 million.
  • Purchases over $1,000 are paid through BuckMe.
  • Savings can’t be withdrawn, but they unlock discounts: 20% off RydeMe ($10,000), 20% off garages ($100,000) and 10% off liveries ($500,000, Premium account).

Early game (Chapters 1 and 2)

  1. Selling cars to Moses: from the Moses mission on. Steal untracked cars far from his garage and bring them in. It is the fastest early method.
  2. Mugging pedestrians and robbing registers: small per hit, but instant.
  3. Thrillbillies and Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab (Billy): good payouts in Port Gellhorn.
  4. Hit Avery Pawn and Hit Kingpin Bowling: optional Chapter 2 robberies; at the bowling alley bring sticky bombs and take all 3 valuables.
  5. Impounded Vehicle and Veteran Hunted (Jason only): extra options to reach the $15,000 for Prepare for Vice City.
  6. Afternoon Delight: $10,000 in one go.

Who to sell to: Brian's lockup opens with code 7700 (you visit it in Lab Fees) and takes drugs, jewelry and marked bills; Brian pays later, so check Jason's mailbox. Donnie pays more for product from his own lost packages. In Chapter 2, Moses buys untracked cars (about $1,000 to $2,000 each, with roughly a 2-minute cooldown): scan them with your phone before bringing them in.

How not to lose what you earn

  • If you die or get busted, you lose the cash you carry and weapons you don't own.
  • Floor stash: Jason's bedroom has a box under the floorboards where you can deposit and withdraw any time.
  • Mailbox: mission pay and fence money often arrive there. Check it whenever you leave home.
  • BuckMe: unlocks with mission 12 of Chapter 1; after that, deposit at any ATM. Shops take up to $999 in cash.
  • Watch the chapter change: Chapter 1 cash doesn't carry into Chapter 2; only BuckMe savings do. Save your first $5,000 early.

Hidden loot worth grabbing

  • The criminals' warehouse (South Vice-Dale County, east of Hamlet): take out the criminals, grab the bag on the table (over $6,000), open the safe inside and loot the vans. About $10,000 in total, plus drugs and an exclusive card. Take it home before taking risks.
  • Sell your cards: at pawn shops like Vice City Pawn, in the collectible cards tab. Some are worth $2,700 and selling them doesn't remove your collection progress.
  • Free weapons: parked police cars have weapons in the trunk, so you can skip Ammu-Nation. See how to do it without getting caught.

More money secrets in easter eggs.

In Vice City (Chapter 3 onward)

  • Banks: 14 small and 8 large with a vault (vaults open after the optional mission That's Where The Money Is…).
  • Payday loans: 7, with a teller and a back safe.
  • Smash 'n' Grabs: 35 between jewelry stores and pawn shops; the back safe pays more.
  • Gang operations: 39 across 9 gangs; they give narcotics, jewelry and watches.
  • Armored trucks: Yasmani sells security van tips.
  • Randy's Wish List: 16 sports cars to order with the Key Cloner; it covers the $200,000 vehicles-sold goal.
  • Stores: 72 convenience stores, 14 liquor stores and 23 fast food places to rob.

Fences: who to sell to

  • Brian (Key Lento): narcotics and Chapter 1 loot.
  • Dunk (Port Gellhorn): from Slam Dunk on.
  • Moses (Port Gellhorn): untracked cars.
  • Randy (Balsero): luxury cars.
  • Yasmani (Little Cuba): jewelry and marked bills; he pays best for laundering.
  • Tyrone (La Perle): product from the Kitty Litter labs.
  • Collectible cards sell at pawn shops for $20 to over $1,000, without losing collection progress.
  • Cocaine from Cocaine Flamingos also sells to fences.

What’s worth buying

  • The 8 Vice City Real Estate garages (about $160,000) to store and register cars.
  • Long guns and ammo at Ammu-Nation; only the 6 Expanded stores sell sniper rifles and rocket launchers.
  • Mod shop upgrades; spending on guns, mods and tattoos is the fastest route to the Treat Yo Self trophy ($100,000).
  • A gym membership to raise Strength and Cardio.
  • BuckMe savings goals for their permanent discounts.
