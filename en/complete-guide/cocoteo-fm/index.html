Cocoteo FM in GTA 6: songs and hosts

GTA 6’s modern Latin music station and the songs attributed to it.

Confirmation levelOfficialOfficialOfficialOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Genre

Modern Latin music: bachata, merengue, dembow and reggaeton.

Official

Hosts

El Martillo (Bad Bunny) and La Gata (RaiNao).

Official

Preview songs

Rockstar shows three songs from the station on its music page: EoO, Si Es Trucho Es Trucho and Canalla.

Official

The rest of the list

The other 17 songs come from an image shared by fans. Rockstar has not confirmed them.

Official

How this list is labeled

The three preview songs Rockstar shows are marked Official. The rest are marked Community. Macacoa 2000 is also track 11 on the official album, but it is not confirmed as part of this station.
