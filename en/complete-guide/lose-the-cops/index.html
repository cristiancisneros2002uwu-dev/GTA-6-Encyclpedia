How to lose the cops in GTA 6

The 6 stars, heat and red icons explained, with the steps to shake off the police.

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

30-second summary

1. Break line of sight: alleys, tunnels, parking garages or duck into a building.

2. Leave the red search area shown on the map.

3. Change what the police know about you: clothes, car, weapon, or split Jason and Lucia up.

If you keep the same description, you can be recognized again even after the chase ends.

How wanted stars work in GTA 6

GTA 6 has 6 stars (one more than GTA V). Police only respond when a crime is reported: a witness, an alarm or a stolen car's tracker. And they don't chase a dot on the map, they hunt a description of you.

StateWhat it looks likeWhat to do
Cops respondingCrime reported noticeFlee before they arrive: it's your best chance to get away clean.
Suspect unknownHollow starsThey don't know who you are. Leave without drawing attention.
In pursuitSolid white starsThey can see you and know who you are. Break line of sight and slow them down by shooting tires, not to kill.
Visual lostFlashing starsThey're searching your last known location. Hide and keep moving out of the radius.
HeatRed stars and a red zone on the mapYou escaped, but they're still looking for your description there. Change it or stay away.

The red icons and how to clear them

Icons under your stars show what the police know. Each one clears separately, and a single match is enough to get you recognized.

Clothes hangerYour outfitChange your whole outfit. Keep a spare set in your car's trunk.

Blood on your clothes and exploded dye packs also give you away. Shower at your safehouse to wash off blood.

How to lose the cops step by step

  1. Break line of sight: take narrow streets, go behind buildings, through alleys, underpasses or underground parking. If a helicopter is on you, a tunnel helps.
  2. Use quiet roads: less traffic means fewer crashes and less chance of getting boxed in.
  3. Watch the lights at the edge of the screen: they show where the police are coming from. Steer away to avoid roadblocks.
  4. Use Focus: cops no longer appear on the minimap; Focus helps you track them.
  5. Hide: your player arrow tells you when you're hidden. Bushes, woods, ladders, unlocked doors, or duck down in a stopped car (hold Circle/B).
  6. Leave the red zone and don't go back while you still match the description.
  7. Change your description: clothes, car, weapon and split the couple until no icons remain.
  8. At 1 or 2 stars, staying out of sight for a short while is usually enough. From 3 stars, actively escaping works better.

Before, during and after a job

Before

  • Keep a spare outfit in the trunk.
  • Put your mask on before going in.
  • Check for patrols, cameras and witnesses.
  • Deposit your cash at an ATM: if you die or get arrested, you lose it.

During

  • Disable alarms if you can and don't shoot display cases.
  • Stop anyone calling the police.
  • Destroy the CCTV recording.
  • Aim without firing: people often flee without a fight and the wanted level stays low.

After

  • Break line of sight and hide.
  • Ditch the getaway car, especially modern ones (they may have trackers).
  • Use an older car you can hotwire.
  • Leave the red zone, change everything, take off the mask and stow your weapon in the trunk.
  • Stay away from the hot area until the heat fades.

Pay 'n' Spray

A respray at a Pay 'n' Spray clears the vehicle recognized icon. Note that some shops refuse cars depending on the tracker installed. If yours is refused, swap cars.

What you lose if you die or get busted

  • Lost: the cash you're carrying, a small BuckMe fee, weapons you don't own and most likely any loot bags you had.
  • Kept: your owned weapons, from your personal vehicle's trunk or an Ammu-Nation locker.
  • Tip: deposit cash at an ATM before any risky job.

Free weapons from police cars (and how to get away with it)

Parked police cars carry weapons in the trunk. Find one with no officers nearby and as secluded as possible, put on a mask, cap and sunglasses, pick the trunk (the alarm goes off), grab the weapons and run. You can get long guns, a handgun or body armor. Stow them in your car and use the steps above to lose the wanted level.

What changes at high wanted levels

  • Helicopters and K9 units join. Dogs track you relentlessly: keep moving.
  • At 3 stars or more, escaping in a regular car is very hard; a motorcycle or running through alleys works better.
  • Officers follow you onto rooftops if they saw where you went.
  • They try non-lethal takedowns first; shoot back and they turn lethal.
  • Shooting at helicopters can make them pull back. Killing cops escalates the response and pushes your criminal profile toward Violent.
  • At 6 stars, the search zone can cover the whole map.
