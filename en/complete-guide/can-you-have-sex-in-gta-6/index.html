Can you have sex in GTA 6?

Everything about sex in GTA 6: with whom, how to unlock it, which mission has it and what changed from GTA 5.

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Yes, you can have sex in GTA 6, but only between Jason and Lucia: how to unlock it, which mission has a scene, why prostitutes are gone and what you can do at strip clubs.

Short answer

  • Yes, but only between Jason and Lucia. There are no other partners and no dating other characters.
  • To do it whenever you want you need the relationship maxed: Hot Together (level 4 of 4). See the relationship guide.
  • There's a scripted scene in Coming Home (Chapter 1), when Lucia gets out of prison.
  • Prostitutes are gone: Rockstar removed them for the first time since GTA III.
  • At strip clubs you can pay for a private dance ($300) and tip, but it doesn't go further.

How to do it with Lucia (or Jason), step by step

  1. Raise the relationship. Jason and Lucia's relationship has 4 levels: Running On Empty, Not So Hot, Crazy In Love and Hot Together. The option only unlocks at the last one. What raises it most: greeting, hugging, holding hands (R2/RT while walking), buying food, answering calls and texts, going to the gym, beach or diving together and robbing as a couple. Full guide in the relationship guide.
  2. Check your level. It's on the first tab of the pause menu. Each time it goes up a heart with a «+» appears on screen.
  3. Find your partner. With the character you control, walk up to Jason or Lucia (home is easiest).
  4. Pick the option with two hearts. When you get close the interaction menu appears; the option marked with two hearts starts the intimate scene.
  5. Watch the scene. It's optional and the camera cuts away at the key moment (it happens off-screen). It can't be triggered at any time: if the option doesn't show, you're not at Hot Together yet or the moment doesn't allow it (for example, during a mission).
  6. Keep the relationship up. The level also drops if you ignore your partner or spend a lot of time apart; if it falls below Hot Together, the option disappears until you raise it again.

Which missions have a sex scene?

Coming Home (Chapter 1, mission 13): when Lucia gets out of prison and they go back to Jason's house, there's a scripted intimate scene. It always happens, regardless of the relationship level or earlier choices.

Beyond that, the story has other couple scenes between Jason and Lucia, always in cutscenes and without showing genitals. The relationship level changes their tone: at Hot Together they're steamier and the couple can spend the night together in the bedroom; at Running On Empty there's no flirting or intimacy.

To reach Coming Home with a high relationship, prepare the house first: buy groceries, flowers and the necklace, and pick up Lucia's clothes in the Chapter 1 optional missions (details in the relationship guide).

Are there prostitutes in GTA 6?

No. Rockstar confirmed you can no longer hire prostitutes in GTA 6, a feature that existed since GTA III. It doesn't matter whether you play as Jason or Lucia, roam at night or progress the story: it never unlocks.

The reason: the game centers on Jason and Lucia as a couple, and Rockstar chose which features returned based on whether they fit their story. There are no booty calls or side relationships either; some characters flirt with Jason in missions, but nothing more happens.

Nudity and age ratings

  • There's nudity in the background of some missions and at a nudist resort southwest of Jason's house, reachable on foot.
  • You can't roam Leonida naked: the most you can strip to is underwear.
  • Jason is shown nude from behind in a shower scene (in Japan he wears a black thong).

FAQ

Can you have sex with other characters?

No. The only couple is Jason and Lucia; there's no dating or relationships with other characters.

Can you have sex with strip club dancers?

No. Only private dances with tips.

How many times can you do it?

As often as you like while the relationship is at Hot Together and the moment allows it.

Is there any benefit?

It helps keep the relationship high, which changes the tone of story scenes.

Why doesn't the option show up?

You're not at Hot Together yet, or you're in the middle of a mission. Check the level in the pause menu and keep doing things together.

Will prostitutes come back in an update?

Rockstar hasn't said anything. For now they don't exist in the game.
