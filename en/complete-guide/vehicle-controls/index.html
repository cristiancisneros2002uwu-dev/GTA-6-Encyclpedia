GTA 6 vehicle controls: headlights, high beams and signals

How to turn on the lights, use high beams and everything else in the car on PS5 and Xbox.

Quick answers

Turn on the headlights: press Circle (PS5) or B (Xbox) while in the car.

High beams, automatic lights or lights off: hold D-pad left to open the vehicle menu and go to Headlights.

Turn signals: tap D-pad left or right. They switch off on their own after the turn.

How to turn on the headlights and high beams

  1. Quick shortcut: in the car, press Circle (PS5) or B (Xbox) to switch the lights on and cycle them.
  2. High beams, automatic or off: hold D-pad left to open the vehicle menu. Push the right stick up to move from the radio to the car options, open Headlights and choose Normal, Full beam, Automatic or Off.
  3. Automatic mode: the car turns its lights on and off based on time of day and weather. The easiest option if you don't want to manage them.
  4. Hiding trick: with the car stopped, hold Circle / B and your character ducks down in the seat. Handy for staying out of sight of the police.

Turn signals and hazard lights

  • Left signal: a quick tap on D-pad left. Right: a quick tap on D-pad right.
  • They turn off on their own once you finish the turn; tap the same direction again to cancel early.
  • Keep the tap short: holding it opens the radio and vehicle menu.
  • Hazard lights: hold D-pad left, push the right stick up, move with R1 / RB to Hazards and confirm with X / A. If the icon doesn't change, close and reopen the menu: it's usually just a visual glitch.

Everything in the vehicle menu

  • Open it by holding D-pad left. The radio shows first; push the right stick up for the car options.
  • Headlights: normal, full beam, automatic or off.
  • Hazards (both signals at once).
  • Windows: roll them up or down.
  • Radio: change stations with L1/R1 or LB/RB. You can also listen to the radio on foot through your phone.

Useful driving tips

  • Leave the engine running (double-tap Triangle / Y) when you need to get in and out fast, like during a robbery.
  • Police cars: click the left stick to turn on the siren; hold it to sound the horn.
  • Nitrous: in Settings › Controls you can choose whether nitrous is held or tapped.
  • Tracked cars: modern cars may have a tracker, which makes them easy for the police to find. Older cars can be hotwired and are better getaway cars.
  • Luxury cars: you need to clone their electronic key before you can take them.

The left stick pitches the nose and is inverted by default. Change it in Settings › Controller › Inverted Controls (aircraft movement).

Useful driving settings

  • Control schemes: Default, Classic and Left-Handed (Settings › Controller).
  • Vehicle shooting assist and automatic holster in vehicles are in Settings › Controls.
  • Adaptive Speed Limiter (PS5): on by default; turn it off if the trigger feels like it's holding the car back.
  • Camera follow speed while driving: Settings › Camera › Land & Water Vehicles.
  • You can map the touchpad (PS5) or View button (Xbox) to the camera in Settings › Controller.
  • Kilometers instead of miles: Settings › User Interface › Units of Measurement › Metric. In the same menu you can switch weight to kilograms, temperature to Celsius and the clock to 24-hour.
