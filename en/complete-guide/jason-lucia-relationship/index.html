How to win over Lucia in GTA 6

The 4 levels of Jason and Lucia's relationship, what raises it, what lowers it and what you get.

Confirmation levelOfficialVisualSeen in demosCommunityUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

The short answer

Spend time with Lucia outside missions: greet her, hold hands, buy her food, answer her calls and do things together, robberies included.

Every time the relationship goes up, a heart with a '+' appears on screen. What hurts it most is ignoring her and spending long stretches apart.

The 4 relationship levels

LevelWhat happens
4/4 · Hot TogetherThe top level. Scenes get steamier and they can spend the night together in their bedroom.
3/4 · Crazy In LoveLots of flirting and more time together.
2/4 · Not So HotThings get awkward and more platonic.
1/4 · Running On EmptyThe lowest level: no flirting or intimacy in scenes. They don't break up; they stay partners in crime.

The level rises and falls throughout the story depending on how you play; it isn't tied to missions. You can check it on the first tab of the pause menu, and the partner menu (on the D-pad) is where you interact with her.

What raises the relationship

  • Greet her every time you meet up.
  • Show affection: hold L2/LT and press Circle/B to hug or kiss.
  • Hold hands: hold R2/RT while walking together.
  • Pay for food or drinks at bars and restaurants.
  • Answer her calls and texts, and message her when you're apart.
  • Do activities together: gym, beach, zoo, scuba diving, kayaking, pool, mini golf and sports.
  • Rob together: stores, banks and jobs as a pair count too.
  • Say yes when she wants to hang out at home, even for a few seconds or going to bed: the boost is instant.
  • Check in after something big, like a major job or a chase.
  • Paint the bedroom and fix up the house.

Get the house ready before she's out (Chapter 1)

For the first hours you only play as Jason. Use them to prepare her return: what you do here raises the relationship before Lucia gets out of prison.

  • Fix up the house Brian lets Jason use and buy groceries.
  • Feng Shui: pick up Lucia's clothes from Andy before heading home.
  • Feng Shui: buy her a necklace (an optional errand before Coming Home).
  • Coming Home: dance with Lucia at the bar; it can raise the relationship several times.
  • Coming Home: during the pool game, defend her from the Aviators to earn a heart.

What lowers the relationship

  • Ignoring her calls and texts.
  • Spending long stretches apart.
  • Abandoning her while you're out together.
  • Hurting her.
  • Making the wrong choices in some scenes.

Neglect can cost you options like holding hands or kissing, but they come back once you spend time together again. Strip clubs don't raise or lower it.

Rewards for a strong relationship

  • Golden Hour: holding hands for 4 hours in total unlocks this filter with a golden tint and widescreen bars.
  • Dollhouse: spending 8 hours with your partner unlocks this filter.
  • Videotape: complete 50 activities with your partner.
  • Faded Glory: rob 50 stores with your partner.
  • The relationship also shapes how the story plays out.

See how to activate them in the cheats list.

Tips to win Lucia over

  • Play as both. Switch to Lucia now and then and look after Jason too: both sides count.
  • Do the small actions every time you're together: greeting, holding hands and treating her to food.
  • If she suggests something pricey, pay for it: it's one of the biggest boosts.
  • Go on dates: beach walks, the zoo, scuba diving or kayaking.
  • Before a long job, top up the relationship with small gestures so a bad outcome hurts less.
