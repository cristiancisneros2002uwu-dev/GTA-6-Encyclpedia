NPCs, wildlife and interiors in GTA 6

Everything you need to start and progress in GTA 6.

NPCs

Crocodiles, alligators, flamingos, dogs and birds. Jason can pet a dog. There is a list of 48 animals on the Wildlife page.

Official

Interiors

Doors, refrigerators, light switches, windows and elevators.

Official
