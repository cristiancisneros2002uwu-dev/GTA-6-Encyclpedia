GTA 6 tips and strategies

Combat, police, robberies, vehicles, partner play and saving: practical tricks to play better.

Combat

  • Combat Focus (LT + RT): slows the action and marks weak points. Use it on groups; with an assault rifle you can drop 5 enemies in a single use.
  • Cover (RB): move from cover to cover and don’t expose yourself while reloading.
  • Melee: block when the enemy rolls their shoulder; counter right after the block.
  • Limbs: shooting arms and legs incapacitates without killing and improves your criminal profile.
  • In vehicles: aim with L1, shoot with R1 and press L3 + R3 for slow motion.
  • Healing: always carry 5 Zombix and armor before heists or gang operations.

Police and wanted level

  • When you see the search area, get out of it as fast as you can; crouching in shadows helps you stay unseen.
  • Dirt roads and shortcuts are better than highways for losing patrols.
  • Vault robberies give 4 stars; the tunnel north of Ambrosia is a good escape route.
  • Respray your car at a Pay 'n' Spray and change clothes to lower recognition. In Vice City they accept cars with a standard tracker.
  • Shooting out police tires ends long chases.
  • If your profile is very violent, police will be more aggressive: play smart.

Robberies and heists

  • Stores: threaten, take the register and leave. In the Breakfast Run robberies each shop has 1 minute.
  • Partner robberies: three stores in a row without losing your stars earn the Retail Therapy trophy.
  • Silent jewelry jobs: with the Snap Gun (Yasmani’s jobs, Chapter 3) go in through the back door; the safe code is in the back room.
  • Banks and payday loans: the Dye Pack Jammer (Credit Check) keeps bills from getting stained; then you launder them with fences.
  • Gang operations: take every key valuable; they often hide luxury watches.
  • Armored trucks: they can be opened with sticky bombs; get away fast.

Vehicles

  • Scan everything you see with WAiNK (D-pad up): it counts for the encyclopedia and 100%.
  • Trackers can be none, standard or advanced. Only sell untracked cars to Moses.
  • To register a car as personal, respray it first; each garage has limited slots.
  • The Slim Jim opens cars without breaking windows and the Key Cloner is for Randy’s jobs.
  • To uncover the map quickly, nothing beats a plane.

Partner play

  • D-pad down opens the partner menu: commands, handing over items and more.
  • Switching characters is instant when they are together; apart it takes a few seconds.
  • Errands and choices in missions like Coming Home or Day Release improve the relationship.
  • Working out together at the gym raises both characters’ Strength at once (Personal Best trophy).

Exploration and activities

  • Use the binoculars to study animals: you need 100 for 100%.
  • There are 18 meditation spots: they count toward a trophy and toward 100% (15 of 18).
  • Collectible cards sit on walls and tables indoors and in yellow phone boxes or tool boxes outdoors.
  • Cocaine Flamingos (black-and-white bill) drop cocaine you can sell.
  • Under the Bridge stunts are the quickest to complete.

Saving and planning

  • Save manually at the start of every chapter and before Recalibration, Checking Out and the final mission.
  • Keep a separate save slot before trying cheats.
  • Deposit before any dangerous mission: pocket cash is lost on death.
  • If a mission breaks (for example dying on the bike in Checking Out), restart it from the menu.
