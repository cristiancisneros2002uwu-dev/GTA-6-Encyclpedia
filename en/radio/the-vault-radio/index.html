The Vault Radio

The Vault Radio: GTA 6's secret electronic station, how to unlock it and its full tracklist.

About the station

The DJ mixes from The Vault, Vice City's home of underground electronic music. It's the 23rd station and it doesn't show up on the radio until you unlock it.

How to unlock The Vault Radio

  1. Reach Vice City and mark The Vault on the map: it's in the center, south of the Jack of Hearts club and west of NINE1NINE.
  2. Go in through the front entrance and look for a red poster with a pink gas mask.
  3. Open the WAiNK camera on your phone (D-pad up) and scan the poster with R1 / RB.
  4. Done: the station stays unlocked for good, in the car and on your phone.
