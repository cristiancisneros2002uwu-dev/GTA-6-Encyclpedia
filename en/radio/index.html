22 stations

Rockstar unveiled six stations with celebrity hosts on October 8, 2026; the rest were confirmed with the game. There are also 2 collectible unique playlists and podcasts on the phone.

The original score is by Justice; their single 'Flower in a Hurricane' features in the launch trailer.
