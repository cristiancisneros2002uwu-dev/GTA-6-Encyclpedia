'67 Transgressor: quick answers

How do you get the '67 Transgressor in GTA 6?

It is an Ultimate Edition bonus you get through the Find Cars for Wyman quest. Not every version of the game includes it.

Is the '67 Transgressor in every edition of GTA 6?

No. It is an edition bonus. The Ultimate Edition content also lists 5 exclusive shops, Jason's and Lucia's custom weapons, the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the '55 Vapid Stanier, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy and the Find Cars for Wyman quest.

Does the '67 Transgressor count toward any trophy?

Yes. It counts toward Whip It Good (discover 100 vehicles) and toward the 200-model 100% goal. You scan vehicles with WAiNK by holding Up and R1, and you do not need to drive them.

What kind of car is the '67 Transgressor?

It is a muscle car. GTA 6 has 34 muscle cars in total, and you can see all of them on the Cars page.

Where it fits in the game

The Ultimate Edition adds a bundle of extras on top of the base game, and the Find Cars for Wyman quest is how you get the '67 Transgressor. Not every version of the game includes it, so check your edition before you go looking for it.

If you are chasing completion, the car is one more model for your checklist: the Whip It Good trophy asks for 100 scanned vehicles and the 100% goal asks for 200 models. See how to get Whip It Good, the edition and content updates and the full list of muscle cars.
