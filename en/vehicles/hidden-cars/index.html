GTA 6 hidden cars and where to find them

Special vehicles hidden in garages and mansions around Leonida, with their location and whether they have an alarm or tracker.

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Hidden cars in GTA 6 and where to find them: Paul Walker's Eclipse, Scooby-Doo's van and more, with alarm and tracker info.

These cars don't show up on the street: they're kept in specific garages, driveways and alleys. Check whether they have an alarm or tracker before stealing them, since that decides whether you can keep them. Some don't always spawn; if it isn't there, come back at another time.

Hidden cars (6)

Paul Walker's green Eclipse

A Fast & Furious tribute: the green Maibatsu Penumbra FF, based on the Mitsubishi Eclipse from the movie.

Where: A villa garage on Vespucci Island, near Vice Beach.

How: Get into the villa, break through to the garage, smash the car window and hotwire it.

What happens: The car is yours. Open the trunk: there's a snake waiting for you.

Official

Classic Pfister Comet at a luxury mansion

A magenta retro Comet with green rims, parked at the driveway of a mansion in Vice City's wealthy area.

Where: Vice City's luxury residential islands, in the driveway of a mansion with an enterable interior (usually empty at night).

How: Walk onto the property. The keys usually aren't there, so you'll have to steal it; since it's an old car, you can break in with no trouble.

What happens: It's yours to drive. Note: it has an advanced tracker, so it's good for a joyride but not for selling or keeping without the police finding it.

Vehicle page: See vehicle page

Official

Imponte Ruiner Turbo with nitrous, next to Jason's house

A black muscle car with an exposed engine and factory nitrous, hidden right next to where you start.

Where: Next to Jason's house, in Mariana County (the Keys).

How: Find the Ruiner Turbo parked at the neighboring house. It's locked and has an alarm, so break in and get away quickly.

What happens: The best part: it has no tracker, so you can keep it. The nitrous is strong; brake before corners.

Vehicle page: See vehicle page

Official

Classic Vapid roadster on the gardeners' island

A 1930s teal roadster kept inside the garage of a mansion on a private island.

Where: A private island in northern Vice-Dale County, near the Leonard County line. The car is inside the mansion's garage.

How: Go in through the back, from the waterfront garden: the front has security and gardeners who will give you away.

What happens: It's unlocked with no tracker and no alarm, so you can drive off with no trouble.

Official

Vapid Hustler (1932): the flame hot rod

A black 1932 hot rod with pink flames and an exposed engine, kept in a house garage in Vice City.

Where: A house garage in Vice-Dale County (Vice City).

How: Go into the garage at night. The catch: two cars are parked in the driveway blocking the exit, so you'll need to squeeze it out or move one.

What happens: It has an alarm and a standard tracker: use it for a ride, or deal with the tracker before trying to keep it.

Vehicle page: See vehicle page

Official

The Scooby-Doo van (Bravado Youga XL 1979)

A green and yellow 1979 Youga XL that looks just like Scooby-Doo's Mystery Machine.

Where: An alley in downtown Vice City, in Vice-Dale County.

How: Head to the alley and get in. It doesn't always spawn: if it isn't there, come back at another time of day.

What happens: It has no security and no tracker, so you can keep it.
