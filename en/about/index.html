About GTA 6 Encyclopedia

An independent GTA 6 encyclopedia, in English and Spanish.

What it is

GTA 6 Encyclopedia brings together guides, characters, the map, vehicles, weapons, missions and cheats for Grand Theft Auto VI, set in the fictional state of Leonida. It is available in English and Spanish.

How we label every fact

Every fact carries a label (Official, Visual, Seen in demos, Community or Unconfirmed) so you know how well it is confirmed. Details are in the verification policy.

Independence

We are an independent fan site, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto is a trademark of its respective owners.

Transparency

We may include advertising and affiliate links. If present, they will be clearly marked and do not influence what we publish or the confirmation labels.

Contact

Found a mistake or want to suggest content? Write to us at contacto@gta6encyclopedia.com.
