Where to start

  1. Cards: they pay the most early on (pawn shops open in Chapter 2) and 3 sets earn the Don't Touch My Deck trophy.
  2. WAiNK: scan landmarks, curiosities, sculptures and vehicles as you play the story; 100 scans earn You WAiNKer! and 100 vehicles, Whip It Good.
  3. Cocaine flamingos: shoot them when you see them; the cocaine sells and 50 destroyed unlock the inflatable flamingo cheat.
  4. Meditation: do the one at Jason's house in Chapter 1 before moving on, as it may be missable.
  5. Leave robberies, stunts and shooting ranges for after the story: that's when the 100% checklist opens and none of it is missable.

Missable collectibles

  • Victor Maravilla (Los Santos Corkers) and Vicki (Delights Dancers) cards: they're at the Starlet Motel and can't be picked up after Chapter 2.
  • The Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house (Positive Mental Attitude trophy).
  • Other than that, nothing on the 100% checklist is missable.

Cards: all 18 sets (128 located)

There are 480 cards across 18 sets and they sell at pawn shops. We have 128 located so far; each set has its own page with every card's exact spot. Main guide: GTA 6 collectible cards.
