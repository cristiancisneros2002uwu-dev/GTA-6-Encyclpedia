GTA 6 treasure

Shipwreck diving for Kenton, the metal detector and where to sell what you find.

Confirmation levelOfficialVisualSeen in demosCommunityUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

How to get treasure in GTA 6: diving for Kenton, the metal detector, the 7 shipwreck sets, underwater treasure and where to sell it.

Quick answer

  • Treasure is recovered by diving on shipwrecks around the Leonida Keys with a metal detector.
  • It opens with Kenton Rees's Island Dreams mission (Chapter 1) in Key Lento.
  • There are 7 shipwreck treasure sets: 100% needs 5, and This Was No Boat Accident pops when Kenton's strand is done.
  • It's one of the best early money makers: no shooting and no cops.
How to unlockHow to searchTreasure typesSellingTips

How to unlock it

  1. Play the Chapter 1 mission Island Dreams: Jason takes a bachelor party diving on a wreck and finds three treasures with the detector. It pays $400.
  2. Afterwards Kenton texts you to come back to his shop (green KR icon in Key Lento). He gives you scuba gear and the metal detector and lets you use his boat any time.
  3. Dive sites show on the map as a purple chest.
  4. Note: Kenton goes away for a while after Chapter 1 and returns between Chapters 2 and 3. Calling Brian as Jason in Chapter 2 can bring him back sooner.

How to use the metal detector

  1. Take the boat to the purple chest and jump in: the scuba gear goes on automatically in the water.
  2. Open the inventory with L1/LB and pick the detector with the right stick.
  3. Aim with L2/LT or R2/RT and follow the signal: the light goes from red to green when you're close.
  4. When it beeps green constantly, move in and dig the treasure up (a chest icon appears).
  5. The detector points to one treasure at a time; don't leave the wreck until you have them all.

Where to sell treasure

  • Shipwreck treasure goes to Kenton: sell each set when it's done and the next one opens.
  • Doubloons are bought by Vice City Pawn (which also takes cards and meteorites). Pawn shop list in the cards guide.
  • More ways to earn in the money guide.

Tips

  • Start as early as you can: it's risk-free money from Chapter 1.
  • Dive in daylight: visibility is much better underwater.
  • Sweep slowly with the detector: the signal is directional.
  • Watch your oxygen and surface before it runs out.
  • Study fish while you swim: they count toward the 100 animals for 100%.
  • Track it under Progress › Characters › Kenton Rees.
