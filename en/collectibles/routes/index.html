Recommended routes: GTA 6 collectibles

What to collect, complete and what you get.

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

Recommended order

  1. During the story, scan vehicles with WAiNK, study animals and rob 50 shops as you pass them.
  2. Chapters 2 and 3: start Moses, Randy, Kenton and Bret as soon as they unlock; Randy covers the $200,000 in vehicles.
  3. Chapter 1: don't miss the meditation at Jason's house (Positive Mental Attitude trophy).
  4. After the credits: turn on the map filters (Robberies, Activities and Exploration) and clear one county at a time.
  5. Under the Bridge is the fastest stunt type to reach 300.
  6. Leave challenges for last: many complete themselves.
  7. Vaults give 4 stars; escape on a fast bike and swap vehicles.
