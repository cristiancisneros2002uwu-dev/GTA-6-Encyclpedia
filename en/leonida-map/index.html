Vice City

A big Miami-inspired city: beaches, nightlife, businesses and tourism. Named places: VC Port, Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Bayside, the Effluvia bar and a Starfish Island sign.

Official

Leonida Keys

Keys and islands, with water, boats and smuggling.

Official

Grassrivers

Leonida’s wetlands, with airboats and alligators. Rockstar calls them the state’s untamed jewel.

Official

Port Gellhorn

The forgotten coast: a former tourist destination of cheap motels, closed attractions and empty strip malls.

Official

Ambrosia

Leonida’s heartland of industry and traditional values, with a sugar refinery and a biker gang.

Official

Mount Kalaga

A national park at the northern edge, with hills, rivers and forest.

Official

Notice

These are the regions Rockstar has highlighted, not every neighborhood, town and road. There is no confirmed official size either.
