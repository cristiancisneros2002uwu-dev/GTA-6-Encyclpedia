GTA 6 strip clubs: the high heel icon

Where NINE1NINE, Delights Cabaret and Jack of Hearts are, what a dance costs and how each club works.

GTA 6 strip clubs 👠: locations, prices, private dances and which club is worth your money.

Quick answer

There are 3 strip clubs in Leonida: NINE1NINE and Jack of Hearts in Vice City, and Delights Cabaret in Port Gellhorn.

On the in-game map they're marked with a high heel icon 👠. Jack of Hearts has its own icon: a heart with a crown.

NINE1NINE is the closest at the start, Delights is the cheapest and Jack of Hearts opens through the story.

The 3 clubs

NINE1NINE

Vice City's luxury spot: half nightclub, half strip club, with private VIP lounges. It's the fanciest and the priciest.

Where: North Downtown, just south of the big highway interchange and right next to The Vault club. About 5 miles from Jason's house in Key Lento.

Inside: Bar, dance floor, private VIP lounges and an ATM. No stage dances.

Prices: $300 private dance and $50 tips.

Tip: Besides talking to a dancer, you can book a private dance with the person at the VIP lounge entrance.

Official

Delights Cabaret

A run-down roadside cabaret with local talent and several stages. The cheap option with more character.

Where: Port Gellhorn (Kelly County), along Interstate 404 on the east side of town near Freedom Tattoo. Look for the red neon sign.

Inside: Several stages, a bar, an ATM and a pool table in the middle where you can challenge a local opponent.

Prices: $40 private dance, $10 tip and $5 stage tips.

Story: In Chapter 2 a story beat takes you there with Vicki, and some staff already recognize Jason.

Official

Jack of Hearts

Boobie Ike's club, a Vice City legend. It's tied to the story and the music scene: the Only Raw Records studio is inside.

Where: North end of Crosstown, Vice City, south of Bayside.

How to get in: It's locked at first. It opens during Chapter 3, when the story takes you to Boobie and Dre'Quan.

Inside: Bar, ATM and the Only Raw Records recording studio.

Icon: On the map it isn't a high heel but a heart with a crown.

Official

How strip clubs work in GTA 6

  1. Bring cash. Dances are paid in cash; every club has an ATM inside if you need it.
  2. Greet first. Walk up to a dancer and hold L2 / LT to look at and greet her.
  3. Ask for a private dance. Then use the interaction button (hold Triangle / Y) to pay and go to the private area.
  4. Tip during the dance to trigger extra animations. At Delights you can also tip whoever is on stage.
  5. Order a drink at the bar with the Order prompt; you can tip the bartender too.
  6. At Delights, play pool: there's a local opponent you can challenge.

Tips

  • Quickest: NINE1NINE, the closest at the start and open from day one.
  • Cheapest: Delights Cabaret; a private dance is $40 versus $300 at NINE1NINE.
  • With Lucia: visiting a strip club doesn't raise or lower Jason and Lucia's relationship. See what does.
  • Out of money? Use the club's ATM before ordering another dance.
  • GTA 6 is rated M (17+) and these clubs are adult content.

Other clubs that aren't strip clubs

  • The Vault: electronic music club next to NINE1NINE. A good place to go dancing with Lucia, and home to the poster that unlocks The Vault Radio.
  • Effluvia: a rooftop bar in Vice City.
  • Rusty Anchor: a bar in the Keys, near where Jason starts.
