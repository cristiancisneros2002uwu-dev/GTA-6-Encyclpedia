Hunting, rare and unique in GTA 6

Huntable animals, rare variants, unique animals and Bret's Target Decks.

Confirmation levelOfficialUnconfirmedWhat do they mean?

How to hunt

Use Focus (L3 + R3) to see vital spots and aim for the head or heart. Bret only accepts animals in Pristine or Good condition. Hunting for Bret is Jason only.

Recommended weapons: Wortham W-22 (small game), Hawk & Little Mediator (foxes, coyotes, bobcats) and Shrewsbury SM-69 (big game).
