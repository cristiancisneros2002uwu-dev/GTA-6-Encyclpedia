Privacy policy

What information we handle when you visit GTA 6 Encyclopedia.

What data we collect

GTA 6 Encyclopedia has no user accounts or forms, and we do not ask for personal data to browse. We only receive your information if you email us.

Technical data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web server, GitHub may log technical data about your visit (such as IP address and browser) under its own privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google servers when a page opens.

First-party cookies

The site does not set its own tracking cookies.

Advertising and affiliate links

We may show third-party ads (for example, Google AdSense) and affiliate links to stores. These third parties may use cookies or identifiers to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage or turn off Google personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com and learn about more options at aboutads.info.

Affiliate links are marked as such. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

If you write to us

We use your email only to reply. We do not share it or use it to send you advertising.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete any messages you sent us by writing to contacto@gta6encyclopedia.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. Last updated: November 17, 2026.
