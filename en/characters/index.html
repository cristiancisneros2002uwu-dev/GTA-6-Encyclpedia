Protagonists (2)

Jason Duval

Army veteran working for Brian Heder in the Keys. He leads Chapter 1 and is one of the two playable characters.

Lucia Caminos

Released from Hamlet Prison at the start of the story, she teams up with Jason. She leads the prologue and Chapter 2.
